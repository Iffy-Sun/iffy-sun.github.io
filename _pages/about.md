Welcome to my site! I am a Quant Analytics Associate working in Card Corrections at JPMorgan Chase & Co. in Wilmington, Delaware. I hold an M.S. in Statistics from the University of Delaware, an MBA, and a bachelor's degree in Public Finance.

My work focuses on using SAS and SQL to investigate and resolve credit card issues involving interest, fees, pricing, promotional offers, and account settings. I translate business problems into data requirements and programming logic, identify affected accounts, calculate refunds and interest, and prepare account adjustment files.

I work across the full correction process, from researching historical business rules and integrating data from multiple sources to validating results, documenting methods, and reconciling outcomes after execution. Previously, I supported Card Pricing & Interchange Analytics at JPMorgan Chase and worked in internal audit at Zhongyuan Bank.

My technical skills include SAS, SQL, R, and Python. I am interested in statistical programming and reproducible data analysis, with an emphasis on accurate results, clear documentation, and reliable workflows.
